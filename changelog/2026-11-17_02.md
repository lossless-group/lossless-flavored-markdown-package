## Why Care?

Notes repeat themselves. The same policy, the same definition, the same
checklist turns up in five places, and the copies drift. Obsidian's answer is
the embed: write it once, then `![[Google Maps#Google Maps API managing policies.]]`
shows that section wherever it's needed. On our sites that line came out as
literal text. Now it comes out as the section itself.

## What's New?

- **`![[Note#Heading]]` on its own line** shows that section of another
  document: its heading, its text, its subsections, up to the next heading at
  its level. `![[Note]]` shows the whole document; `#A#B` reaches into a
  section inside a section.
- **It reads as part of the page,** with a small "From *Google Maps*" line and
  an "Open original" link, so a reader knows it's borrowed and can go to the
  source. That's the reference component, `CrossReferenceContentEmbed`, for
  Astro sites.
- **Your site says where documents live.** LFM never reads files; you pass a
  `load` function that returns a document's markdown and link.
- **Nothing silently disappears.** A missing document or section still shows a
  quiet note and the link, and the build can report it.

## How It Was Checked

Ten new tests cover sections, subsections, both label orders (`#Heading|Label`
and `|Label#Heading`), slug matching, whole documents, missing targets, image
embeds left alone, and a note that embeds itself. All 310 tests pass. See
[Cross-reference embeds](https://github.com/lossless-group/lossless-flavored-markdown-package/blob/master/docs-v/cross-reference-embeds.md).
