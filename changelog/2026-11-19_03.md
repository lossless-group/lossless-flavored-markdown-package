## Why Care?

Everyone publishing through LFM today writes in Obsidian, where `%%…%%` is how
you leave yourself a note. Until now LFM printed those notes as text.

## What's New?

- `%%comments%%` are gone from the page, inline or wrapping whole blocks.
- On by default; `comments: false` turns it off.
- A guide: [Comments](../docs-v/comments.md).

## Under the Hood

Obsidian's rules, kept on purpose:

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| `%%` inside code | Text, not a comment |
| An unclosed `%%` | Hides the rest of the document |
| A list item that was only a comment | Removed, not left as an empty bullet |
