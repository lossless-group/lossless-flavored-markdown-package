## Why Care?

Some of our changelog entries and context docs run to dozens of sections. The
spec on directives has 44. Until now the only way through them was scrolling.

## What's New?

- **A table of contents on every changelog entry and context doc.** On wide
  screens it's a rail on the left, lined up with the site's mark, and it
  highlights the section you're reading. On laptops it folds into a menu
  button; on phones it's a bar at the top naming the current section.
- Pages with fewer than three sections don't show one.

## Under the Hood

- The same component every Astro Knots site ships, copied from cathode-site,
  with the splash's colors and fonts.
- Built from the outline Astro returns when it renders the page, so the links
  match the ids on the headings exactly; it never reads the page itself.
- The splash moved to Astro 7 earlier today, with every other dependency at its
  latest.
