---
title: Eyebrow, heading, subheading
kind: feature
plugin: lfm-heading-blocks
order: 10
note: >-
  Markdown gives you one line where editorial practice has always used three.
  `$$` and `&&` bind by adjacency to the heading between them — a blank line
  breaks the binding and the markers revert to ordinary text.
---

$$ Portfolio Operations
## Every email from a portco, filed as PDFs
&& Two passes, inventory before export — so you can verify coverage
